Behind Jakab All Trades

Hi, I’m Lucy.

I’m a photographer, host, furniture refinisher, wife, and mom based in Pendleton, South Carolina. Jakab All Trades brings together the different parts of my work and the things I love to share.

I love finding beauty in a family’s connection, a well-loved piece of furniture, or a small space with plenty of character. My faith and family are at the heart of it all, and I’m glad you’re here.
